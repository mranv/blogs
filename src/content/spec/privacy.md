## Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** January 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Anubhav Gain ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you visit **mranv.pages.dev** (the "Site").

### Information We Collect

#### Email Address (Newsletter Subscribers)

If you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address. This is used solely to send you:

- Confirmation emails (double opt-in)
- Welcome emails
- Newsletter broadcasts about new blog posts

We use a **double opt-in** process — you must confirm your subscription via email before receiving any content.

#### Analytics

We do not use third-party analytics services. Cloudflare (our hosting provider) may collect basic server logs (IP addresses, request timestamps) for security and operational purposes, as governed by [Cloudflare's Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

### How We Use Your Information

- To deliver newsletter content you requested
- To respond to unsubscribe requests promptly
- To maintain the security and integrity of the Site

### Data Storage

- Email addresses are stored in **Cloudflare KV** (key-value storage) on Cloudflare's global edge network
- Emails are sent via **Cloudflare Email Routing** from `newsletter@techanv.com`
- No payment information is collected (we do not sell anything)

### Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

You have the right to:

- **Access** — Request a copy of your data
- **Delete** — Request deletion of your email from our system
- **Unsubscribe** — Cancel your subscription at any time via the unsubscribe link in any email, or by visiting `/api/unsubscribe?email=your@email.com`

To exercise any of these rights, contact **Iamanubhavgain@gmail.com**.

### Data Sharing

We **never** share, sell, or rent your email address to third parties. Period.

### Cookies

This Site does not set tracking cookies. Theme preferences (light/dark mode) are stored in your browser's `localStorage` and are not transmitted to any server.

### Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

### Contact

Questions? Email **Iamanubhavgain@gmail.com**.
